What these forms do

Username/password accounts and learning roles

When MongoDB sign-in is enabled, KR7Tech stores your username, salted scrypt password hash, account ID, learning role, account status, sign-in timestamps and plan records privately in MongoDB. Readable passwords are not stored or returned, and credentials are not saved to browser local storage. Create account and Login require only a username and password, not an email address or OTP. Username-only accounts do not yet provide email password recovery. An internal non-deliverable identifier is used for database compatibility, not email notifications. Active sessions last up to seven days; the browser receives an HTTP-only Secure cookie on HTTPS. Sign-out revokes the current session on the server. Network and login identifiers are keyed-hashed for rate limiting. New accounts start as Free Students; signup does not purchase a plan or grant Admin access. Already-signed-in legacy users can add username/password login to the same account, preserving their purchases. These records are separate from marketing consent; accounts are not automatically linked merely because an email matches.

Newsletter and masterclass interest

With your consent, KR7Tech stores your email, optional name, selected list, and consent timestamp in its private database. Lists are separate. Joining is not an account, a payment, a Pro unlock, or a confirmed event booking. This application does not currently send newsletters or confirmation emails.

Feedback and publication

Feedback stores your display name, chosen learning path, rating, comment, and consent. It is not public by default. Only comments with separate publication consent and owner approval are returned to the public courses page. Community comments are not represented as verified purchases or job outcomes.

Assistant and optional contact requests

Suggested FAQ answers are predefined and do not call an AI provider. They remain available without sharing contact details. If you request support follow-up and consent, your name, email, optional mobile number, and consent timestamp are stored in the site's private database. This does not enroll you in marketing, schedule a call, or grant Pro. Contact values are not saved to browser local storage by the widget.

Abuse prevention and hosting

The server uses a keyed hash of the network address for a database-backed submission limit. Raw addresses are not written into the form tables by this application. Hosting and database providers may maintain their own operational logs. These forms do not add advertising trackers or store entered email addresses in browser local storage.

AI interview membership and usage

AI interviews send your answers to the configured model provider after consent. The membership database records your account ID, session ID, timestamps, turn count, a hash of the interview settings, and request ownership state to enforce plan access and Go's daily allowance. That usage table does not store your answers, questions, or audio. The account-linked usage records remain in the site's database until removed under the owner's retention policy; session expiry ends access but does not itself delete usage history. The model, speech, hosting, and database providers have their own data practices. Do not include sensitive information in practice answers.

Change your mind or request removal

Use the owner's support contact below to withdraw interest or request removal of a submission. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. Requests require appropriate verification by the owner; these forms do not expose other people's records or provide an anonymous record-deletion endpoint.

Payments and purchase history

KR7Tech stores your account-linked plan, purchase reference, provider, amount, currency, payment status and relevant timestamps to manage membership and support requests. Checkout is handled by the selected payment provider; this application does not collect card security codes or store complete card numbers. Provider and hosting services operate under their own privacy practices. Purchase history is returned only to the signed-in account that owns it.

Saved subjects and review progress

Saved subjects, recent learning and reviewed-item checkmarks are stored in the browser you use. They are not synchronised across devices and do not determine paid membership. Your membership is checked against account records when accessing paid content.
