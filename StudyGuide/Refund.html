Payments and refund reviews

I paid but my plan is not active

Log in with the account you used for checkout, then open My purchases and refresh. Confirmation may arrive after the payment provider returns you to the website. Do not pay again while the first charge is being checked. If your provider confirms payment but access remains unavailable, contact support with the purchase reference.

I see a duplicate or unexpected charge

Contact support with the purchase references, provider name, approximate dates and amounts. Include the relevant receipt details while hiding unrelated information. We review the records with the payment provider before confirming a resolution.

How do I request a refund review?

Contact support from the published address and explain the issue, your username and purchase reference. Refund requests are reviewed individually against the purchase and the issue reported. A request does not itself approve or execute a refund. This website does not provide an automatic refund button or a general money-back guarantee.

When does an approved refund arrive?

If a refund is approved and processed, confirmation and timing depend on the payment provider and bank. Support will explain the available status; no instant credit or fixed bank-processing time is promised here.

Will I be charged again automatically?

No. The current Go and Pro checkout is a one-time payment. It does not create a recurring subscription. A later plan purchase is a new payment and must be chosen separately.

What should I never share?

Do not send your password, card security code, full card number, OTP, session cookie, private API key or database credentials. Support does not need these to investigate a purchase.
